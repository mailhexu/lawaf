# Symmetry-first LWF effective models

This page records the design required before implementing a full anharmonic model in Lattice Wannier Function (LWF) coordinates. It expands the mandatory construction sequence into implementable steps, each with its mathematical condition, procedure, lawaf entry point, pitfalls, and acceptance criterion.

## Principle

The Wannier functions must be symmetry-adapted before the energy model is fitted. Making only the polynomial coefficients symmetric is insufficient: a non-symmetric LWF gauge gives coordinates that do not transform as a valid crystal representation.

The final model has the form

$$
E(\mathbf Q)=E_{\mathrm{harm}}(\mathbf Q)+E_{\mathrm{anh}}(\mathbf Q),
$$

where $\mathbf Q$ contains LWF amplitudes $Q_i(\mathbf R)$. The harmonic and long-range dipole parts remain exact; the anharmonic residual is fitted to DFT or MLIP energies and forces.

## Three symmetry levels

Three distinct requirements must hold, in this order:

1. **Retained-subspace symmetry.** For every space-group operation $g$,

$$
P(g\mathbf q)=S_g(\mathbf q)P(\mathbf q)S_g(\mathbf q)^\dagger.
$$

If projector covariance fails, no gauge rotation can make all retained functions symmetric without changing or enlarging the target subspace.

2. **Individual LWF symmetry.** Centres form a complete Wyckoff orbit, and the gauge obeys Sakuma's covariance condition

$$
U(g\mathbf q)D_W(g,\mathbf q)=\widetilde D(g,\mathbf q)U(\mathbf q),
$$

where $\widetilde D$ is the representation of the underlying phonon basis and $D_W$ is the desired induced representation of the LWF basis.

3. **Energy-model symmetry.** Only then is the anharmonic energy restricted to invariant functions of $Q_i(\mathbf R)$, built from the same validated representation $D_W$.

## Notation and conventions

Fix these before starting; every later step silently depends on them:

- Space group $G=\{(W|\boldsymbol\tau)\}$ from `spglib` with an explicit `symprec`; record the standardized cell and the atom map of every operation.
- Mass-weighted displacements $u_{\kappa\alpha}=\sqrt{M_\kappa} \Delta r_{\kappa\alpha}$, so the symmetry action is unitary (symmetry-equivalent atoms carry equal masses; isotopic disorder breaks this).
- The q-space symmetry operator, acting on mass-weighted displacement vectors, with atom permutation $g\kappa$, Cartesian rotation $W$, lattice shift $\mathbf h_g(\kappa)$, and Bloch phase. One consistent convention is the spgrep-modulation eigenmode representation

$$
\Gamma^{\mathbf q}_{\kappa'\alpha';\kappa\alpha}(g)
=e^{-i W\mathbf q\cdot \mathbf h_g(\kappa)} \delta_{\kappa',g\kappa} W_{\alpha'\alpha}.
$$

- The dynamical matrix covariance follows:

$$
\mathcal D(g\mathbf q)=S_g(\mathbf q) \mathcal D(\mathbf q) S_g(\mathbf q)^\dagger.
$$

- Fourier phase, mass, and Wigner-Seitz $R$-degeneracy (`Rdeg`) conventions identical to those used by lawaf's harmonic `k_to_R` transform.

Beware phase-convention mismatches when mixing eigenvector sources. For example, prototyping against `spgrep-modulation` on BaTiO$_3$ found the concrete relation $v_{\mathrm{lawaf}}(\kappa)=e^{+2\pi i \mathbf r_\kappa\cdot\mathbf q} v_{\mathrm{spgrep}}(\kappa)$; with the opposite sign the overlap residual jumps from $\sim10^{-14}$ to $\sim0.7$. Derive and unit-test the convention once, centrally.

## The seven-step construction sequence

### Step 1 — Choose a symmetry-compatible Wyckoff orbit and site-symmetry irrep

**Goal.** Decide where the LWF centres sit and how many LWFs exist, before touching any gauge.

**Construction (Sakuma).**

1. Pick a candidate site $\mathbf q_{\mathrm{site}}$ (Wyckoff position). Its site group (stabilizer) is

$$
G_{\mathbf q}=\{(W|\boldsymbol\tau+\mathbf T)\in G: W\mathbf q_{\mathrm{site}}+\boldsymbol\tau+\mathbf T=\mathbf q_{\mathrm{site}}\}.
$$

2. Pick a site-symmetry irrep $\beta$ of $G_{\mathbf q}$ with matrices $d^{(\beta)}(W)$ and dimension $d_\beta$. The representative LWFs $W_{i1}$, $i=1,\dots,d_\beta$, satisfy

$$
g_q W_{i1}=\sum_{i'}d^{(\beta)}_{i'i}(W_q) W_{i'1}.
$$

3. Let $\{g_{j0}\}$ be coset representatives; the orbit sites are $\mathbf q_j=g_{j0}\mathbf q_{\mathrm{site}}$, $j=1,\dots,N_{\mathrm{orbit}}$. The full LWF set is generated by induction:

$$
W_{ij}=\hat g_{j0} W_{i1},
\qquad
N_{\mathrm{LWF}}=N_{\mathrm{orbit}}\times d_\beta.
$$

**Compatibility constraint.** The phonon branches retained in the downfolding window must decompose, at every high-symmetry $\mathbf q$, into exactly the little-group irreps contained in the band representation induced from $(\mathbf q_{\mathrm{site}},\beta)$. Check:

- decomposition of every retained degenerate block into little-group irreps (Step 4 machinery);
- compatibility along high-symmetry lines connecting those points;
- multiplicity bookkeeping: $N_{\mathrm{LWF}}$ must equal the number of retained branches.

**Obstructions are real.** If the induced band representation does not match the retained subspace, a symmetric exponentially localized basis with those centres does not exist for that isolated subspace (elementary band representation / topological obstruction, Cano et al.; Bradlyn et al.). Remedy: enlarge or shift the frequency window, add branches, or choose a different orbit/irrep. Do not proceed and "fix it later".

**lawaf hook.** `WannierParams` currently carries no site/orbit/irrep fields. The natural extension is explicit fields (`site_symmetry=True`, `wyckoff`, `site_irrep`) so the choice is recorded in the LWF netCDF metadata next to `centers`.

**Acceptance.** A declared orbit (list of centres closed under $G$), a declared irrep label and dimension, $N_{\mathrm{orbit}}\times d_\beta=n_{\mathrm{wann}}$, and an irrep decomposition of the window that matches the induced representation at all high-symmetry points.

### Step 2 — Ensure the retained phonon subspace is space-group covariant

**Goal.** The set of retained branches must be mapped into itself by every operation.

**Procedure.**

1. At every mesh $\mathbf q$, build the projector $P(\mathbf q)$ onto the retained eigenvectors (lawaf's weight-function selection must yield a definite retained set per $\mathbf q$).
2. For every $g\in G$ with $\mathbf q\mapsto g\mathbf q$ on the mesh, compute

$$
r_g(\mathbf q)=\bigl\|P(g\mathbf q)-S_g(\mathbf q)P(\mathbf q)S_g(\mathbf q)^\dagger\bigr\|.
$$

3. Star consistency of the disentanglement window: frequencies are invariant under $g$ (eigenvalues of $\mathcal D(g\mathbf q)$ equal those of $\mathcal D(\mathbf q)$), so a purely frequency-based window is automatically star-consistent **except** at degeneracies straddling the window edge. There, band identity is ambiguous; resolve by rounding degeneracy groups wholly in or out, never splitting a degenerate block across the window.

**Pitfalls.** Splitting a degenerate block at the window edge (silent covariance break); using a symmetry-reduced mesh without star closure; forgetting that NAC-treated $\Gamma$ blocks mix LO/TO content — retain the same polar treatment on both sides of the check.

**Acceptance.** $\max_{g,\mathbf q} r_g(\mathbf q)$ below tolerance (e.g. $10^{-10}$ relative) with every degenerate block either fully retained or fully discarded.

### Step 3 — Construct symmetry-adapted mass-weighted phonon trial functions

**Goal.** Initial projections that already carry the site symmetry, so the gauge iteration starts inside the symmetric manifold.

**Procedure.**

1. Build localized mass-weighted displacement patterns $\{t_{i}(\mathbf r)\}$ around the representative centre — e.g. centre-atom displacements and short coordination-shell displacement combinations (the Rabe-Waghmare shell construction).
2. Project onto the site irrep with the character projector (scalar irreps) or the Neto matrix projector (multidimensional irreps):

$$
\mathcal P_\beta=\frac{d_\beta}{|G_{\mathbf q}|}\sum_{h\in G_{\mathbf q}}\chi_\beta(h)^* S_h .
$$

3. Induce over the orbit: $t_{ij}=\hat g_{j0}t_{i1}$.
4. Bloch-sum to each irreducible $\mathbf q$ and project onto the retained subspace: $\tilde t(\mathbf q)=P(\mathbf q) t(\mathbf q)$, then orthonormalize (SVD).

**Shortcut.** `spgrep-modulation` `Modulation.eigenspaces` / `project_eigenmode_representation` (Neto method) returns symmetry-adapted phonon vectors per little-group irrep directly. These can seed the projection instead of hand-built shell patterns; align phase conventions first (see Notation).

**Acceptance.** Trials at the representative site transform exactly as $d^{(\beta)}$ under $G_{\mathbf q}$ (residual $\sim10^{-12}$), and orbit-induced trials have nonzero projection onto the retained subspace at every irreducible $\mathbf q$.

### Step 4 — Treat degenerate phonon subspaces as complete blocks

**Goal.** Never assign symmetry labels to individual eigenvectors that a numerical diagonalizer returned in an arbitrary gauge.

**Facts.** Within a numerically degenerate eigenspace (same eigenvalue to within `degeneracy_tolerance`), `eigh` output is gauge-arbitrary; lawaf's `align_all_degenerate_eigenvectors` is a deterministic alignment heuristic, not a symmetry construction. Symmetry acts on the whole block:

$$
D_{\mathrm{ph}}(g,\mathbf q)=V_{\mathbf q}^{\dagger}S_g(\mathbf q)V_{\mathbf q},
\qquad g\in G_{\mathbf q},
$$

with $V_{\mathbf q}$ any orthonormal basis of the block. This matrix representation is gauge-covariant (basis changes conjugate it), so its irrep **content** is well defined even though individual columns are not.

**Procedure.**

1. Group retained eigenvectors into degenerate blocks with an explicit tolerance.
2. For each block at each irreducible $\mathbf q$, build $D_{\mathrm{ph}}(g,\mathbf q)$ for all $g\in G_{\mathbf q}$ and decompose the block into little-group irreps with character projection

$$
\mathcal P_\beta(\mathbf q)=\frac{d_\beta}{|G_{\mathbf q}|}\sum_{g\in G_{\mathbf q}}\chi_\beta(g)^* D_{\mathrm{ph}}(g,\mathbf q)
$$

(or the Neto matrix projector, as in `spgrep-modulation`).
3. Verify the decomposition matches the induced-representation content demanded by Step 1. A mismatch is an obstruction, not a fitting problem.
4. Keep whole blocks together through every subsequent operation.

**Pitfalls.** Splitting blocks at accidental near-degeneracies (choose tolerance relative to the spectral scale); treating conjugate $-\mathbf q$ points independently under time reversal; assuming the little group of $\Gamma$ applies at boundary points (nonsymmorphic factor systems differ there).

**Acceptance.** Every retained block labeled by little-group irreps; irrep multiplicities reproduce the induced band representation; no block split across a window edge.

### Step 5 — Enforce gauge covariance across each $\mathbf q$ star during localization

**Goal.** One gauge at irreducible points; all star members derived, never independently optimized.

**Covariance equation.** With $U(\mathbf q)$ the retained-eigenvector-to-LWF gauge and $\widetilde D(g,\mathbf q)=S_g(\mathbf q)$ the phonon-basis representation,

$$
U(g\mathbf q) D_W(g,\mathbf q)=\widetilde D(g,\mathbf q) U(\mathbf q).
$$

**Procedure.**

1. **Little-group fixed-point constraint.** At each irreducible $\mathbf q$, the gauge must satisfy

$$
U(\mathbf q)=\widetilde D(h,\mathbf q) U(\mathbf q) D_W(h,\mathbf q)^{\dagger},
\qquad \forall h\in G_{\mathbf q}.
$$

Starting from the Step-3 trials, iterate the symmetrization average

$$
U'\leftarrow\operatorname{SVD}\left[\frac{1}{|G_{\mathbf q}|}\sum_{h\in G_{\mathbf q}}\widetilde D(h,\mathbf q) U D_W(h,\mathbf q)^{\dagger}\right]
$$

until the residual

$$
\varepsilon(\mathbf q)=\sum_{h\in G_{\mathbf q}}\bigl\| \mathbf 1-U^{\dagger}\widetilde D(h,\mathbf q) U D_W(h,\mathbf q)^{\dagger}\bigr\|
$$

converges (Sakuma). $D_W(h,\mathbf q)$ is supplied by the site-irrep choice of Step 1; at $\Gamma$ it reduces to $d^{(\beta)}$ blocks on the orbit.

2. **Star extension.** For star members, derive — do not re-optimize:

$$
U(g\mathbf q)=\widetilde D(g,\mathbf q) U(\mathbf q) D_W(g,\mathbf q)^{\dagger}.
$$

3. **Constrained localization.** During spread minimization, symmetrize the gradient/gauge update over the group and reimpose the covariance constraint after every sweep. In Sakuma's notation the disentanglement update is averaged as

$$
\widetilde Z(\mathbf q)=\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g}\widetilde D(g,\mathbf q)^{\dagger} Z(g\mathbf q) \widetilde D(g,\mathbf q),
$$

and the gauge step enforces $\widetilde U(g\mathbf q)D_W(g,\mathbf q)=\widetilde D(g,\mathbf q)\widetilde U(\mathbf q)$ throughout. Wannier90's `sitesym.F90` (`sitesym_symmetrize_u_matrix`, `sitesym_symmetrize_gradient`) is the electronic reference implementation of exactly this pattern.

4. **Conjugate points.** Time reversal ties $U(-\mathbf q)$ to $U(\mathbf q)^*$; enforce this too.

**Pitfalls.** Running unconstrained MLWF first and hoping to symmetrize afterwards (does not work — the symmetric manifold is not reached by post-projection of a generic gauge); letting an unconstrained iteration drift centres off the orbit (Wannier90 `guiding_centres` addresses centre drift only, not the full representation); enforcing only $G_{\mathbf q}$ at $\Gamma$ and forgetting star members and nonsymmorphic operations at the zone boundary.

**Cost note.** The constrained minimum can have a larger spread $\Omega$ than the unconstrained one. That is expected and acceptable: symmetry is the constraint; report both spreads.

**Acceptance.** $\varepsilon(\mathbf q)$ at machine precision for all irreducible $\mathbf q$; star-derived $U$ reproduce the trials' symmetry; spread converged under the constraint.

### Step 6 — Validate real-space LWF transformation residuals and representation closure

**Goal.** Prove the produced LWFs really transform as claimed before any energy fitting.

**Checklist.** For every operation $g\in G$ and the chosen supercell $G_S$:

- **Representation closure.** $D_W(g_1g_2)=D_W(g_1)D_W(g_2)$ within tolerance, including nonsymmorphic translations and lattice-shift bookkeeping.
- **Real-space transformation.** With LWF displacement vectors $W_i(\mathbf R)$ (mass-weighted, as stored in `wannR`/`wann_disps`),

$$
g W_i(\mathbf R)=\sum_{i'}\bigl[D_W(g)\bigr]_{i'i} W_{i'}\bigl(\mathbf R'_g(i,\mathbf R)\bigr),
$$

measure the least-squares residual of the predicted permuted/mixed patterns against the transformed actual patterns. Relative Frobenius residual $\lesssim10^{-8}$ (tunable).
- **Centre orbit.** Centres map exactly as the Wyckoff orbit of Step 1; the stabilizer of each centre acts as $d^{(\beta)}$.
- **Orthonormality** in the mass-weighted metric.
- **Harmonic covariance.** For the real-space harmonic matrix and the induced $T_g$ on $(i,\mathbf R)$,

$$
T_g^{\mathsf T}H(\mathbf R) T_g=H(g\mathbf R),
$$

equivalently $T_g^{\dagger}H T_g=H$ for the invariant form.
- **Coverage tests.** $\Gamma$; zone-boundary points (nonsymmorphic factor systems); multidimensional degeneracies; conjugate $\mathbf q$ pairs; NAC on and off.

Record every residual in the LWF netCDF as first-class metadata. A symmetry validation that is not persisted will not survive the next regeneration.

**Acceptance.** All residuals below declared tolerances; on failure, the report names the failing operation and check (that distinguishes wrong centre map vs. gauge mismatch vs. tolerance issue).

### Step 7 — Only then fit symmetry-invariant anharmonic energy terms

**Goal.** Fit the residual energy surface in a coordinate system with a proven representation.

**Fit recipe.**

1. Derive the finite-supercell representation $T_g$ on $(i,\mathbf R)$ from the validated LWFs (least-squares identification against transformed displacement patterns, or directly from $D_W$ plus the affine index map).
2. Build the invariant basis by Reynolds projection of monomials,

$$
\mathcal R_n m(\mathbf Q)=\frac{1}{|G_S|}\sum_{g\in G_S}m(T_g\mathbf Q),
$$

or equivalently the fixed space of the induced symmetric-power representation; cross-check invariant counts against the Molien series coefficient of $t^n$.
3. Keep the harmonic and analytic dipole sectors exact; fit only the residual with

$$
\mathbf u=\mathbf M\mathbf Q,
\qquad
\mathbf g_Q=\mathbf M^{\mathsf T}\mathbf F_{\mathrm{atom}},
$$

using teacher energies and projected forces jointly, with mass/sign/unit conventions documented once.
4. Strain, if included, is another representation sector: $\epsilon\mapsto W\epsilon W^{\mathsf T}$, projected with the same machinery.

**Why a symmetric polynomial cannot repair a broken gauge.** Symmetrizing energy coefficients imposes $E(\mathbf Q)=E(T_g\mathbf Q)$ for a group action $T_g$ that, if the gauge was never validated, is a *fiction*: the physical map $\mathbf u=\mathbf M\mathbf Q$ is then not equivariant, $\mathbf u(T_g\mathbf Q)\neq S_g\mathbf u(\mathbf Q)$. The consequences are structural, not cosmetic:

- symmetry-equivalent physical distortions correspond to different $\mathbf Q$ points, so the fitted surface either double-counts or excludes them;
- projected forces inherit the inconsistency, so force-fitting spreads the error back into every coefficient;
- any later gauge cleanup of the LWFs invalidates the fitted coefficients, because the coefficients were adapted to one particular (symmetry-breaking) coordinate map.

The energy model is only as symmetric as the representation carried by its coordinates. Validate the gauge first; then invariant fitting is safe, cheap, and stable.

## Current lawaf state (implementation prerequisites)

lawaf today: `PhonopyWrapper.solve` diagonalizes with `eigh` plus a deterministic alignment heuristic; `ProjectedWannierizer` takes anchor/basis projectors without symmetry metadata; the phonon downfold path has no symmetry-constrained gauge; the LWF netCDF schema stores `wannR`, `Hwann_R`, centers, `Rdeg` but no representation/orbit/irrep metadata or validation residuals. Missing pieces, in dependency order:

1. $S_g(\mathbf q)$ construction (mass-weighted, phase-convention-tested) — foundation for everything.
2. Subspace covariance check (Step 2).
3. Site-irrep projectors and symmetry-adapted seeds (Steps 1, 3, 4) — `spgrep-modulation` can supply the phonon irreps.
4. Covariance-constrained gauge and star extension (Step 5).
5. Validation residuals persisted in netCDF (Step 6).
6. $\mathbf Q\leftrightarrow\mathbf u$ teacher plumbing with force projection (Step 7 prerequisite; `build_lwf_lattice_mapping_matrix` exists, force projection and the corrected `get_distorted_atoms` return do not).
